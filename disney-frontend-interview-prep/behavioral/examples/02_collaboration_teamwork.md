# STAR template: collaboration / teamwork

Example prompt shape: "tell me about a time you disagreed with a teammate" / "describe
a time you went above and beyond for someone on another team" / "tell me about a
conflict and how you resolved it."

Fill this in with a real story — don't fabricate one. Good candidates: a design/API
disagreement you had to actually resolve (not escalate), a dependency owned by another
team that blocked you, a time someone pushed back on your PR/design and you changed
your mind (or held your ground with a good reason).

- **Situation**: _(1–2 sentences — who were the people/teams involved, what was the
  shared goal or dependency?)_
- **Task**: _(what did you specifically need from them, or they from you?)_
- **Action**: _(what went wrong first, then: the specific thing YOU did to resolve it —
  a conversation you started, a doc you wrote, a compromise you proposed.)_
- **Result**: _(how it actually got resolved — a believable partial win beats a
  suspiciously clean one.)_
- **Learning**: _(the concrete change: e.g. "I now put a written API contract in front
  of a partner team before I start building against it, not after," because that's
  literally what would have prevented the friction in this story.)_

## Common failure mode to avoid

Don't tell a story where the other person/team was simply wrong and you were simply
right, even if that's true — it reads as one-sided. The interviewer wants your role in
causing or resolving the friction, not a case you're relitigating.
