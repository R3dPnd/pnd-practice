# STAR template: guest / customer impact

Example prompt shape: "tell me about a time your work directly impacted a customer/
user" / "how do you think about the end user when making technical decisions" / "tell
me about a time you had to balance technical elegance against what users actually
needed."

Fill this in with a real story — don't fabricate one. Good candidates: a UX/performance
fix you made after noticing real user pain (not just an internal code-quality concern),
a time you pushed back on a feature because of user impact, a case where you had data
(analytics, support tickets, user feedback) that changed a technical decision.

- **Situation**: _(1–2 sentences — what product/feature, and who was the end user —
  a consumer-facing viewer, or an internal user like an editor/ops person?)_
- **Task**: _(what user-facing problem were you specifically trying to solve?)_
- **Action**: _(what you actually built/changed, and why you made that call over
  alternatives — tie it explicitly back to the user's experience, not just "it was
  more efficient.")_
- **Result**: _(the measurable or observed impact — a metric, reduced complaints, faster
  load time, higher completion rate.)_
- **Learning**: _(the concrete change: e.g. "I now instrument a feature's actual usage
  before considering it done, because I learned my assumption about how people would
  use it was wrong.")_

## Common failure mode to avoid

Don't describe a purely internal technical win (a refactor, a faster build, a cleaner
abstraction) dressed up as customer impact unless you can draw a real, specific line
from it to something a user experienced differently. If you can't draw that line
honestly, use a different story for this theme.
